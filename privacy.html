Privacy

Twins Burgers, Palm Beach (Gold Coast). Last updated November 2026.

When you order online we only ask for what we need to prepare your order and hand it over. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your name and phone number.
  • Your order: items, notes (for example allergies or "no onions"), total and pickup time.
  • Whether the order was paid. Card payments are handled by Square; we never see or store your card details.

Why we use it

  • To prepare your order and call your name at pickup.
  • To contact you about your order if something comes up.
  • To keep business and tax records.

We don't sell your details or use them for marketing unless you ask us to.

Where it is stored

Orders are stored with our online service providers (Supabase for orders, Square for payments, GitHub for this website). Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States. Only the Twins Burgers team can see your order details.

How long we keep it

We keep order records only for as long as we need them for the reasons above, including record-keeping we're required to do.

Your choices

You can ask us what details we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them when we no longer need them. Message us on Instagram @twinsburgers__ or ask at the counter.

On this device we only save the number of your latest order, so you can check its status. It is removed automatically.
